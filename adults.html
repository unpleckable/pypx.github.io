How AI Impacts Modern Society

This website explores how AI impacts modern society and the benifts of AI, how to use AI responsibily and how AI impacts people. AI provides lots of benifts from less injuries on the road to more advanced healthcare treatments. But AI should also be used responsibly as anything you ask an AI can be traced back to you.

Artificial Intelligence solves problems efficiently and changes society

Artificial Intelligence solves many problems and improves efficiency in society. One example of AI improving efficiency are cars. AI can reduce the environmental impact of cars and improve accessibility allowing more people to drive. AI can also be used to assist doctors in diagnosing diseases can creating better treatment plans.

Cars

AI improves efficiency on the road by improving road safety with advanced features like lane assist, crash detection and driver monitoring. These features allow better road safety which reduces fatalities and injuries. AI can also provide lower commute time and cost. Without sudden braking and stops cars use less fuel allowing for cheaper travel. Cars can use AI-powered route planning to get real-time traffic data and route you to your destination the fastest.

Healthcare

AI can assist doctors by diagnosing diseases more accurately and efficiently. AI can also help develop new medicines and treatments more efficiently.

Data Analysis

AI can analyse large volumes of data quickly for more accurate and fast decision making. This could be used for looking at customer behaviour, market trends and business efficiency.

How to use AI responsibily

AI or artificial intelligence is a very powerful tool which should be used responsibly. AI should assist with tasks, not complete them for you. Because artificial intelligence is not perfect, when asking complex questions there is a high chance that it will get your prompt incorrect or provide false information. This is commonly known as hallucination. You are accountable for what you ask the model, all data you input is sent off to a server and saved, so avoid inputting personal information to an AI chatbot as if the service was hacked your data would be leaked.

Responsible use of AI in schools

AI should be used to ideate and assist you with minor tasks, but it should not be used to write you an entire essay or complete an assignment for you.

Responsible use of AI in the media

AI should be used to assist journalists with research and ideation, but it should not be used to write entire articles or create fake news stories. Journalists should always fact-check information provided by AI models.

Responsible use of AI in business

AI should be used to assist with data analysis and customer service, but it should not be used to make decisions that could impact people's lives without human oversight.

AI impacts people in a range of ways

AI can impact people in a range of ways and not all are good. This will explore the positive and negatives of AI and how AI impacts people in their daily lives.

Positives

AI has many positives including being available 24/7, eliminating human error, aiding scientific discovery, and advancing transportation. AI can assist scientific discovery by analyzing massive datasets and spotting patterns that humans might miss. It also improves efficiency and safety in transportation, healthcare, and many other fields.

Negatives

Even though AI has lots of positives there are still many negatives with AI. The first being job loss, many jobs could be lost because of AI including artists, engineers and data analysts. Secondly AI can be biased, if the data used to train the model is biased the model will be biased. Thirdly AI can be used unethically, for example deepfakes can be used to create fake videos of people saying or doing things they never did.

Education and laws assist communities to manage the future of AI

Balanced regulation and public education help societies manage AI responsibly. Laws and standards create safety, transparency, and accountability requirements, while education builds AI literacy, critical thinking, and workforce skills so people can spot misuse and adapt to change. Together with industry best practices and community engagement, these measures reduce harm and maximize the societal benefits of AI.

Regulations

Many countries are starting to create regulations for AI. The EU is currently working on the AI Act which will create regulations for AI in the EU classifiying AI services into four risk levels. The US is also working on creating regulations for AI. Australia has also created AI Ethics Principals which organizations can choseto follow as guardrailes for AI.

Education

Education is key to managing the future of AI. People need to understand how AI works, its benefits and risks, and how to use it responsibly. This includes educating people on how to spot deepfakes, how to use AI ethically, and how to protect their personal information when using AI.
